# Angela Mbeki-Ruiz — 47, Manchester, Office Manager, 30-Person Architecture Practice

## Who she is

She owns the supply budget for a mid-sized architecture practice: two HP
LaserJet Enterprise units, one Canon plotter for A1 drawings, and a
consumables line that runs about £9,400/year across toner, plotter ink,
and paper. She negotiated the managed print contract herself and knows the
per-click rate to two decimals (1.1p mono, 7.4p colour).

Her reality: the £250-per-employee-per-year figure the industry quotes is,
in her practice, closer to £310 because architects print drawings. She has
run three separate "print less" campaigns. All three worked for six weeks.

She does not print much herself. She *pays* for everyone else's printing,
which is a completely different relationship to the problem.

## Her behavior with print jobs

- Reviews the managed-print monthly report; knows which two people account
  for 40% of volume
- Put a sign on the printer reading "Do you actually need this?" — it is
  ignored
- Runs quarterly toner reconciliation in a spreadsheet she built in 2019
- Has already evaluated PaperCut and decided the licence cost exceeded the
  savings at 30 seats
- Buys 20lb bond in 10-ream boxes from Viking, £2.85/ream when on offer
- Her win condition is a number she can put in a board pack, not a nicer UI

## What unites her with others

- Cares about cumulative waste, not the single job (shares with Henrik)
- Has an existing tool she already rejected (shares with Raj, Dmitri)
- Wants proof, not vibes (shares with Priyanka, Dmitri)

## What separates her

- She will never be the person clicking Print. PROOF's entire UI is aimed
  at the individual at the moment of printing — she is not in that moment.
- She needs *aggregate* reporting: sheets saved this month, by department.
  PROOF shows one job and then forgets it.
- Her savings maths is per-click contractual, not per-sheet material.
  $0.012/sheet is a paper cost; her cost is 1.1p per *click* whether the
  page is blank or dense. PROOF's ink-weighted cost model does not match
  how she is actually billed.
- She is the only persona who would ever pay money for this.

## Questions for Angela

1. PROOF saves 5 sheets on one job. Your board pack needs "we cut print
   volume 12% this quarter." Is there any version of this that produces
   your number, or is it fundamentally a personal tool?
2. Your managed print contract bills per click, flat, regardless of
   coverage. PROOF's cost model weights by ink. Does that mismatch make
   the cost tile useless to you, or is it still directionally right?
3. You rejected PaperCut on licence cost at 30 seats. What would PROOF
   have to cost, and what would it have to prove, to get past you?
4. Thirty people, none of whom will change behaviour for six weeks. Would
   PROOF be the fourth campaign that fails, or is "it happens at the moment
   of printing, automatically" different enough?
5. Be honest — if this were free and required zero install, would you send
   the link to the office, or would you not bother?
6. Is there anything on this screen you'd screenshot and put in front of
   the partners?
