Three things are true at once and you need all three.

1. **The comprehension gate is passed.** This is not DOSE. Eight of eight

   about that failure mode — it is solved.
2. **The trust premise is currently broken by the product's own screen.**

   screen right now. For a product whose entire differentiator is measured
   truth, this is the worst possible sentence to ship.

   hero.** The Ink X-ray is the изюминка. Everything else is a competent,
   well-made, *forgettable* utility.

## Raj Shanmugam — 38, managed print services architect

**1. One second?**
Yes. Coverage-based waste flagging with a preview wall.

**2. Would you use it? What closes the tab?**
Professionally I want to be fair to this, because the engineering underneath
is better than the category usually gets. But three questions have to be
answered and none of them are on screen.

**a) Novelty.** Blank-page suppression already exists in PCL and PostScript
drivers. It's shipped for twenty years. It is usually **switched off**, because
it breaks duplex collation — you suppress a blank on the back of sheet 4 and
everything after it flips to the wrong side. So the honest positioning isn't
"nobody tried since 1995," it's *"the driver has this and it's disabled for a
good reason; here's the version that isn't."* That's a better story and it's true.

**b) The duplex question, which the product does not address.** I dropped
sheet 4 of 15 and looked at what the print CSS does: `.page.dropped
{display:none}`. The sheet is hidden. Nothing reflows. Yet the detail panel
tells the user *"Pagination reflows and the sheet never leaves the tray."*
Pagination does not reflow. Nothing reflows. That's the second sentence on
this screen that says something the code doesn't do.

**c) The error bar.** Your coverage is a *pre-print estimate* built on
`GLYPH_DENSITY = 0.16`. The printer measures actual coverage for real and
reports it in the MIB after the job. I'd want to see your number next to a
device-reported number on the same document. If you can show they agree within
a stated tolerance, you have an engineering claim nobody else in this space
has bothered to make.

**3. Missing?**
The real enterprise waste isn't near-empty sheets. It's duplicate submissions,
wrong-tray, and abandoned pull-print jobs. You're aimed at a genuine problem
that is not the big one.

**4. Actively bad?**
The "reflows" sentence, and the 2%-threshold contradiction below. Both are
small strings and both attack the only thing you're selling.

## Is there an изюминка?

**Yes — one, and it is not what you are leading with.**

The **Ink X-ray** is the hook. Not because it's a nice dark-mode skin, but
because of what it is: the document rendered as the *literal geometry the
measurement was computed from*. Every cyan rectangle is a real
`Range.getClientRects()` box that was summed into the coverage number. Every
magenta block is a table rule whose stroke area was counted. It is the rare
case where the beautiful image and the proof of the claim are the same object.

That is a genuinely memorable idea: **"here is your document as the printer
sees it — as ink, not as words."** Fifteen sheets of typographic constellation
with five grey voids in it. You can describe that image to someone over the
phone. You cannot do that with the wall of thumbnails.

Now the harsh half. **Without the X-ray, this is a competent utility with no
magic.** A wall of readable page thumbnails with pink borders on the empty
ones is *correct*, it is *useful*, and it is *forgettable*. It is the thing a
reasonable person would build. Four of your five frames are that thing.

## Does it clear the "interesting / memorable / aesthetic" bar?

**Split verdict, and the split is the actionable part.**

- **Aesthetic: yes.** Real type scale, tabular numerals, a genuine mono/sans
  pairing, optical alignment, no gradients, no glass, no purple, real content
  instead of lorem. It will not be mistaken for vibecoded output. Ife's read
  is that the direction is a known dialect spoken well rather than an original
  point of view — but spoken well is enough to clear this bar.
- **Interesting: only via the X-ray.** Frames 1–4 are a good print preview.
  A judge that has seen a hundred entries will file them as "clean utility."
- **Memorable: currently at risk, for a mechanical reason.** Three duplicate
  frames, one clipped mobile frame, and the best composition in the product
  (the detail view, which *argues* rather than displays) missing entirely.
  You are showing five images and carrying two ideas.

**The concept is not weak.** I was prepared to say it was and the evidence
doesn't support it — eight of eight personas comprehend it instantly, the
numbers are genuinely computed, the X-ray is a real hook, and the restraint on
the environmental claim is the most trustworthy thing on the screen. This is
a better idea than DOSE and it is not a solution looking for a problem;
Dmitri's own number (10,000 sheets/worker/year, 45–65% binned same day)
defeats his own case.

**The execution is what is at risk**, in four specific places: a false sentence
on screen, a broken capture, a clipped mobile frame, and an invisible agentic
layer. All four are fixable in well under an hour, and none of them require
rethinking the product.
